## 1. Purpose

This document defines certification, capitalization, archive, and closure criteria for P3-WS-001.

It creates no certification decision.

It creates no capitalization report.

It creates no archive.

It creates no code, implementation, API, architecture, or Blueprint.

---

## 2. Certification Authority

P3-WS-001 certification must be performed under:

- PROGRAM-003 Engineering Execution Framework;
- Architecture Freeze v1.0;
- Kernel Baseline v1.0;
- PROGRAM-002 certified WS-001 through WS-008 corpus;
- EXEC-001;
- MIG-001;
- MIG-002.

The Certification Board issues the Workstream certification decision.

Allowed certification decisions:

- GO;
- GO WITH RECOMMENDATIONS;
- NO GO;
- BLOCKED.

## 3. Certification Entry Criteria

P3-WS-001 may enter certification only when:

1. all planned lots are complete or formally blocked with accepted disposition;
2. all future Mission Order evidence is inventoried;
3. review evidence is complete;
4. documentary test evidence is complete;
5. verification evidence is complete;
6. Architecture Board checkpoint is recorded;
7. Engineering Board checkpoint is recorded;
8. Certification Board readiness checkpoint is recorded;
9. traceability evidence is complete;
10. capitalization candidates are identified;
11. archive readiness evidence is prepared;
12. no prohibited change occurred.

## 6. Capitalization Criteria

Capitalization may proceed only after certification evidence is complete enough for decision.

Capitalization must record:

- reusable governance findings;
- Mission Order discipline findings;
- traceability findings;
- evidence and documentary test findings;
- Board checkpoint findings;
- risk and blocker lessons;
- recommendations for later PROGRAM-003 Workstreams;
- limits of applicability;
- confirmation that no doctrine, rule, agent, architecture, baseline, API, code, Blueprint, or implementation is modified by capitalization.

Capitalization must not create new doctrine.

Capitalization must not change certified specifications.

---

## 7. Archive Criteria

Archive readiness requires:

- final P3-WS-001 deliverable inventory;
- certification decision evidence;
- capitalization evidence;
- final traceability evidence;
- final verification evidence;
- Board checkpoint evidence;
- SHA-256 evidence for final files;
- confirmation that original sources are not moved, deleted, overwritten, or silently changed;
- confirmation that PROGRAM-002 archives remain unchanged;
- confirmation that Architecture Freeze v1.0 and Kernel Baseline v1.0 remain unchanged.

---

## 8. P3-WS-001 Closure Criteria

P3-WS-001 may be declared CLOSED only when:

1. Certification Board decision is GO or accepted non-blocking status;
2. capitalization evidence is complete;
3. archive readiness evidence is complete;
4. all risks and blockers are closed, accepted, or escalated;
5. no prohibited modification occurred;
6. P3-WS-002 remains unopened until closure is formally recorded.

Closure must be documented by a later authorized closure mission.

This certification plan does not close P3-WS-001.
