## 1. Executive Summary

P3-WS-001 is officially created as the first PROGRAM-003 Workstream.

This Workstream establishes the governance and traceability setup required before downstream construction Workstreams may proceed.

This charter creates no code, implementation, API, architecture, or Blueprint.

---

## 2. Mission

P3-WS-001 exists to prepare PROGRAM-003 construction execution controls.

Its mission is to define the workstream-level governance basis for traceability, evidence obligations, certification gates, Mission Order discipline, baseline preservation, and stop or escalation controls.
