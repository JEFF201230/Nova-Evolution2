## 5. Documentary Coherence

The plan is coherent with the WS-002 Charter because:

- WS-002 is recorded as CREATED, READY TO START, and NOT STARTED.
- WS-002 specifications require later Mission Orders.
- the plan preserves the future deliverable list without producing those deliverables.
- the plan keeps doctrine, mission, report, decision, certification, capitalization, and archive artefacts separate.

The plan is coherent with PROGRAM_002_WORKSTREAMS.md because:

- WS-002 remains the Workstream for Execution Model work.
- later Workstreams retain their responsibilities for Kernel Services, Lifecycle Management, Mission Runtime, Agent Runtime, Workspace Runtime, and Operating System Certification.
- the plan enforces one active Workstream and no overlap.

The plan is coherent with NOVA_EXECUTION_MODEL.md because:

- Squad execution requires a Mission Order.
- agents execute bounded responsibilities.
- Decision Reports are required for unresolved authority or architecture matters.
- reports preserve evidence and do not create doctrine.

The plan is coherent with NOVA_PROGRAM_GOVERNANCE.md and NOVA_KERNEL_DOCTRINE.md because:

- it prevents scope drift into Kernel, Platform, Products, or VEEDDA;
- it does not move Kernel responsibilities;
- it does not introduce business logic into Kernel or Operating System;
- it does not authorize Kernel primitive specification.
