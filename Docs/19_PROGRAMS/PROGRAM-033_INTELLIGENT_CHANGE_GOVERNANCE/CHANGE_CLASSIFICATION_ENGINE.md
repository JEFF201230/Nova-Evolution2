# CHANGE CLASSIFICATION ENGINE

## Purpose

Classify change by risk, scope, urgency, and blast radius.

## Classification Dimensions

- Change type
- Business impact
- Technical impact
- Operational impact
- Security impact
- Rollback complexity

## Decision Rules

- Low risk may proceed to human review.
- Medium risk requires explicit approval.
- High risk requires escalation and stronger evidence.
- Unclassified change is blocked.

## Evidence

- Classification rationale
- Decision threshold
- Exception handling record

## Decision

Change classification defined.
