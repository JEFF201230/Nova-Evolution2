# DECISION INTELLIGENCE ENGINE

## Purpose

Analyze change evidence and produce decision recommendations.

## Inputs

- Mission scope
- Dependency data
- Impact evidence
- Risk evidence
- Policy evidence

## Outputs

- Recommended decision
- Confidence level
- Risk summary
- Required human validation points
- Evidence package

## Rules

- AI recommends; AI does not approve.
- Recommendations must be explainable.
- Recommendations must reference evidence.
