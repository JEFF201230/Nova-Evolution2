# PROGRAM-017 - GOVERNANCE

## Authority

PROGRAM-017 is governed by the NOVA Program Board and the Global PDS Orchestrator, under the certified PROGRAM-016 foundation.

## Governance Rules

- No specialized PDS may self-authorize its own launch.
- No uncontrolled parallelism is permitted.
- Every deliverable must preserve traceability.
- Every downstream artifact must remain compatible with PROGRAM-016 contracts and audit foundations.
- No protected file may be modified without explicit authorization and evidence.

## Bootstrap Rules

- PDS-001 establishes the governance and execution foundation.
- PDS-002 through PDS-012 may start only after the Global PDS issues launch authorization.
- Certification and closure require every specialized PDS to satisfy its exit criteria.

Governance controls established.
