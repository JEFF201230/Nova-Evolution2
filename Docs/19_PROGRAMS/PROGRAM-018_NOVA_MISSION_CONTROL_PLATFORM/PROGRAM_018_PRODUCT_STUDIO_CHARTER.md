# PROGRAM-018

# NOVA Product Studio

## Status

INITIATED

---

# Mission

The NOVA Product Studio is responsible for transforming the certified V0 Product Discovery Package into a complete production-ready product experience.

The Product Studio SHALL NOT redefine the product vision.

The Product Studio SHALL transform the validated experience into world-class product design.

---

# Operating Principle

Research drives Design.

Design drives Prototype.

Prototype drives Validation.

Validation drives Implementation.

No visual decision SHALL contradict the certified Product Discovery Package.

# MVP Delivery Strategy

The Product Studio SHALL operate in accelerated design sprints.

Each sprint SHALL produce a complete, reviewable increment.

Target duration:

- Discovery Review
- Wireframes
- High Fidelity Design
- Interactive Prototype
- Design Certification

The objective is rapid execution while preserving certified product quality.

# Parallel Product Studio Execution Model

## Execution Principle

The NOVA Product Studio SHALL execute through parallel multidisciplinary squads coordinated by a Chief Product Orchestrator.

The objective is to maximize execution speed while preserving product quality, coherence and traceability.

---

## Parallel Squads

The following squads SHALL operate simultaneously:

1. Product Experience
2. User Journeys
3. Information Architecture
4. Conversation & AI Experience
5. Design System
6. Screen Factory
7. Prototype
8. Accessibility
9. VEEDDA Experience
10. Innovation Lab
11. Technical Readiness
12. Quality & Certification

---

## Orchestration Rules

All squads SHALL:

- use the certified PROGRAM-018 V0 Package as the single source of truth;
- work autonomously within their assigned scope;
- resolve non-blocking conflicts through the Chief Product Orchestrator;
- preserve consistency across all deliverables;
- avoid duplication of work;
- synchronize before final integration.

---

## Consolidated Deliverables

The Product Studio SHALL deliver one integrated package containing:

- Product Studio Integrated Report
- Design System Foundations
- Component Inventory
- Screen Specifications
- Responsive Specification
- Interaction Specification
- Motion Specification
- Accessibility Validation
- VEEDDA Experience Validation
- Technical Readiness Report
- Figma Handoff Package
- Final Certification Report

Intermediate fragmented deliveries SHOULD be avoided unless explicitly requested.

---

## Completion Rule

The Product Studio SHALL stop only after:

- all squads have completed their work;
- the integrated package has been assembled;
- certification has been issued;
- the final decision has been recorded.

# Success Criteria

PROGRAM-018 SHALL be considered DESIGN COMPLETE when:

- the V0 package has been fully translated into visual design;
- all certified journeys are represented;
- the prototype is complete;
- accessibility requirements are satisfied;
- implementation handoff is approved.
