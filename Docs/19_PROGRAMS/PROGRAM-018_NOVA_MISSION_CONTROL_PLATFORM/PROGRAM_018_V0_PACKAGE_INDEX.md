## Canonical V0 Experience

NOVA V0 begins with one public question:

**What would you like to achieve?**

The user describes an outcome in ordinary language. NOVA clarifies the objective, proposes a comprehensible plan, identifies assumptions and risks, obtains explicit approval for consequential decisions, coordinates people and AI, shows progress, and produces usable outcomes.

## Public Navigation

The canonical public navigation is:

- Home
- Work
- Decisions
- Deliverables

Search, notifications, help, profile and organization switching are utilities. Internal governance vocabulary remains hidden unless an authorized expert enables Expert detail.

## Required Figma Prototype Flows

1. Create something new.
2. Recover work in difficulty.
3. Conduct analysis or research.
4. Prepare and approve a strategic decision.
5. Produce and export a professional deliverable.
6. Monitor execution and resolve a blocked item.
7. Collaborate with human and AI experts.
8. Complete a critical decision with explicit human approval.

## Scope Boundary

This package authorizes high-fidelity design and prototype validation only. It does not authorize React implementation, API changes, modification of certified baselines or autonomous approval of critical decisions.
