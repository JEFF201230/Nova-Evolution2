# How Humans Naturally Start Complex Work

---

# Purpose

This research program SHALL determine how human beings naturally approach complex objectives before interacting with any software.

The conclusions SHALL become one of the primary foundations governing the future NOVA user experience.

---

# Scientific Question

How do humans naturally initiate complex work?

---

# Research Hypothesis

People do NOT think in terms of software.

People think in terms of intentions, objectives, concerns, constraints and expected outcomes.

---

# Research Scope

The research SHALL investigate:

- how objectives emerge;
- how problems are formulated;
- how people search for information;
- how trust is established;
- how decisions begin;
- how uncertainty is managed;
- how collaboration starts;
- how people ask for help;
- how people delegate work;
- how confidence evolves.

## Scientific Facts

The sprint identified the following converging observations.

### SF-001

Humans begin with an objective.

They do not begin with software.

---

### SF-002

People formulate intentions before they formulate actions.

---

### SF-003

Users naturally describe outcomes rather than processes.

Example:

"I want to launch my company."

Not:

"I want to create a project."

---

### SF-004

People progressively build confidence.

Confidence is rarely immediate.

---

### SF-005

Humans expect guidance during uncertainty.

---

### SF-006

Users naturally reduce complexity into manageable steps.

---

### SF-007

Trust increases when progress is visible.

---

### SF-008

Users expect the system to understand context without unnecessary repetition.

# Executive Research Synthesis

## Sprint Conclusion

The multidisciplinary research sprint reached a strong convergence across cognitive science, behavioural observation, enterprise practices and human-centered design.

Although populations differ significantly in expertise, responsibilities and objectives, they consistently exhibit common behavioural patterns when initiating complex work.

The following findings SHALL become candidates for Knowledge Asset production.

---

## Finding 01 — Objectives Precede Tools

People naturally begin with an objective.

Software is perceived only as a possible means to achieve that objective.

Implication:

NOVA SHALL always start from the user's objective.

---

## Finding 02 — Complexity Creates Anxiety

Users naturally overestimate the complexity of unfamiliar situations.

Visible complexity reduces confidence.

Implication:

NOVA SHALL progressively reveal complexity.

---

## Finding 03 — Humans Seek Confirmation

Before acting, users search for reassurance that they are moving in the right direction.

Implication:

NOVA SHALL continuously provide confidence indicators.

---

## Finding 04 — People Think in Outcomes

Users describe expected outcomes rather than implementation details.

Implication:

NOVA SHALL translate intentions into executable missions.

---

## Finding 05 — Navigation Is Secondary

Users rarely think about menus.

They think about their immediate objective.

Implication:

Navigation SHALL follow the mission rather than expose the software structure.

---

## Finding 06 — Guidance Reduces Cognitive Load

People naturally accept guided experiences when uncertainty is high.

Implication:

NOVA SHALL adapt guidance according to user confidence and expertise.

---

## Finding 07 — Trust Is Progressive

Trust develops through repeated successful interactions.

Implication:

NOVA SHALL progressively earn trust rather than assume it.

---

## Finding 08 — Human Control Remains Essential

Users are willing to delegate execution but remain responsible for important decisions.

Implication:

Critical decisions SHALL always remain under explicit human authority.
