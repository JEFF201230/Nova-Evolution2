## Purpose

P18-MO-001 SHALL serve both as:

- the execution authority for the PROGRAM-018 Product Studio V1 campaign;
- the first operational candidate of the NOVA Canonical Mission Execution Pattern.

The pattern defines how NOVA transforms one governed objective into coordinated parallel missions, consolidated deliverables, integrated validation and one final certification decision.

## Canonical Pattern Sequence

```text
Governed Objective
↓
Mission Order
↓
Parallel Execution Missions
↓
Independent Mission Deliverables
↓
Conflict Resolution
↓
Consolidated Integration
↓
Quality Validation
↓
Certification Decision
↓
Reusable Execution Evidence
Canonical Invariants

Every future implementation of this pattern SHALL:

preserve one Mission Order as the execution authority;
define explicit mission scopes;
identify dependencies before execution;
permit parallel execution only when dependencies allow it;
prevent conflicting modifications to protected artefacts;
consolidate outputs through one controlled integration process;
resolve contradictions before certification;
retain decisions, evidence and validation results;
issue one final governed decision;
stop after the authorized objective has been completed.
Reuse Scope

The pattern MAY be instantiated for:

product discovery;
product design;
research;
architecture;
software implementation;
migration;
audit;
compliance;
certification;
incident recovery;
customer onboarding;
commercial preparation;
organizational transformation.
Pattern Governance

P18-MO-001 remains specific to PROGRAM-018.

Its execution structure MAY serve as the reference candidate for future canonical mission templates.

No future Program SHALL copy this pattern blindly.

Every reuse SHALL declare:

the objective;
the authority;
the activated missions;
the dependencies;
the protected scope;
the expected deliverables;
the validation process;
the stop conditions;
the final decision model.
Certification Boundary

Execution of P18-MO-001 does not automatically certify the Canonical Mission Execution Pattern for portfolio-wide use.

Portfolio-wide certification SHALL require:

successful completion of P18-MO-001;
execution evidence;
conflict and dependency validation;
integration validation;
retrospective analysis;
independent review;
explicit NOVA governance approval.
