# V0 Multidisciplinary Research Synthesis

## Status

COMPLETE FOR V0 PRODUCT DISCOVERY

## 1. Method and Evidence Boundary

This synthesis consolidates PROGRAM-018 internal research and targeted desk research across cognitive psychology, human factors, enterprise and consumer UX, accessibility, AI interaction, service design, information architecture, cultural diversity, visual cognition and product strategy.

The campaign did not conduct new moderated studies with external participants. Consequently:

- published standards and reproduced source requirements are classified as EVIDENCE;
- findings already recorded by RP-001 are classified as INTERNAL MVP FINDINGS;
- design deductions are classified as INTERPRETATIONS or RECOMMENDATIONS;
- untested product behavior is classified as HYPOTHESIS or INNOVATION;
- no synthetic persona is presented as empirical user evidence.

This limitation does not block Figma readiness because the V0 prototype is the instrument required for representative usability testing. It does prevent claims of production usability certification.

## 3. Consolidated Internal Findings

The following RP-001 findings are accepted as V0 design inputs at MVP maturity, not as universal scientific laws:

- people begin with objectives rather than software structures;
- intentions precede detailed actions;
- outcomes are easier to express than implementation processes;
- confidence grows progressively;
- guidance is valuable during uncertainty;
- manageable steps reduce perceived complexity;
- visible progress supports trust;
- relevant context should not require unnecessary repetition;
- important decisions remain under explicit human authority.

## 5. Cross-Disciplinary Conclusions

### 5.1 Cognitive psychology and human factors

Evidence supports reducing memory demands, preserving context, grouping related information, preventing errors and making system status visible. V0 SHALL show one primary next action, a short plan and expandable detail.

### 5.2 Enterprise UX

Enterprise work requires roles, accountability, sensitive information boundaries, auditability and exception handling. V0 SHALL not expose those mechanisms to every user by default, but SHALL preserve them behind role-appropriate views.

### 5.3 Consumer UX

Fast comprehension, direct language, forgiving input and continuity across devices lower entry barriers. V0 SHALL accept natural-language objectives and avoid setup forms before value is visible.

### 5.4 Accessibility

Compliance requires semantic structure, keyboard operation, visible focus, adequate contrast, reflow, alternatives to drag, accessible authentication and error recovery. Accessibility SHALL be annotated at screen and component level.

### 5.5 AI interaction

Trustworthy assistance requires capability disclosure, uncertainty, provenance, correction, feedback, undo where possible and explicit approval for consequential actions. NOVA SHALL never imply that confidence equals truth.

### 5.6 Service design

The experience includes activity outside the interface: people, meetings, source systems, approvals and deliverables. Journeys SHALL display external dependencies and ownership.

### 5.7 Information architecture

Users need stable locations while work remains mission-driven. The solution combines four stable destinations with contextual next actions, rather than either menu-only or chat-only interaction.

### 5.8 Cultural diversity

Directness, hierarchy, risk expression, color meaning, date/number formats and willingness to challenge AI vary by context. V0 SHALL localize formats, avoid idioms, never encode meaning by color alone and allow private or collective review.

### 5.9 Visual cognition

Position, scale, grouping and whitespace should establish hierarchy before color. Persistent red is reserved for blocking or destructive states. Charts SHALL include text summaries and data access.

### 5.10 Product strategy

NOVA differentiates through governed movement from objective to outcome, not through a generic chatbot or a conventional project dashboard. Conversation initiates and explains; structured workspaces make execution, decisions and outputs inspectable.

## 6. Classification Register

### Evidence-backed requirements

- WCAG 2.2 AA target.
- Keyboard-complete operation.
- Plain and understandable content.
- Error prevention for legal, financial and destructive decisions.
- Human oversight and traceable consequential decisions.
- Role-appropriate explanations.

### Assumptions

- Most first-time users can state an objective more easily than select a workflow.
- Four primary destinations are sufficient for V0.
- A combined conversational and structured workspace is preferable to either alone.

### Hypotheses to test in Figma

- H-01: The prompt “What would you like to achieve?” enables at least 80% of representative users to begin without assistance.
- H-02: Users can distinguish Work, Decisions and Deliverables after one guided mission.
- H-03: The proposed confidence presentation reduces uncertainty without creating automation bias.
- H-04: Adaptive modes change detail appropriately without causing disorientation.
- H-05: Critical approval review is comprehensible to non-experts and sufficiently detailed for experts.

### Innovations

- Mission Canvas: a live, plain-language contract between user intent and execution.
- Confidence Lens: separates confidence, evidence coverage, assumptions and unresolved questions.
- Adaptive Depth: changes explanation depth without moving canonical objects.
- Decision Pause: a deliberate, accessible interruption before consequential commitment.
- Deliverable Lineage: connects every output section to sources, assumptions and decisions.

### Contradictions and resolutions

| Contradiction | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| “Hide complexity” versus enterprise traceability | Default to plain summaries; preserve inspectable expert detail. |
| AI initiative versus human authority | AI may propose and prepare; humans approve consequential commitments. |
| Conversation flexibility versus predictable navigation | Conversation is an interaction layer, not the sole information architecture. |
| Personal adaptation versus consistency | Adapt density and guidance, never object names, locations or decision meaning. |
| Speed versus governance | Low-risk reversible work can proceed quickly; critical decisions invoke Decision Pause. |
| Universal experience versus role relevance | One object model with role-based views and permissions. |
| Historic PROGRAM-018 numbering versus current charter | Current PROGRAM-018 charter governs this package; historic strategy documents remain unchanged and are recorded as legacy numbering conflict. |

### Limitations

- No new external participant sessions were conducted in this campaign.
- No production telemetry exists for the proposed V0.
- Screen-reader and switch-device behavior cannot be certified before an interactive prototype or implementation exists.
- Model response quality, latency and cost depend on PROGRAM-017 and runtime implementation choices.
- Organization-specific legal, HR and public-sector requirements require deployment-level assessment.

## 7. Research Validation Plan for Figma

The high-fidelity prototype phase SHALL test all eight canonical flows with participants spanning novice/expert, operational/executive and disabled/access-needs cohorts. Each round SHALL measure task start success, comprehension of status, decision accuracy, recovery, perceived control and terminology comprehension. Findings SHALL update V0.1 before implementation authorization.
