# V0 Product Foundation

## Status

APPROVED FOR FIGMA

## 1. Value Proposition

### Public promise

**Tell NOVA what you want to achieve. NOVA helps you turn it into a clear, controlled path to a usable result.**

### Customer value

NOVA reduces the distance between an ambiguous objective and a governed outcome. It clarifies intent, organizes work, brings in people and AI, exposes uncertainty, protects critical decisions, monitors execution and produces professional deliverables.

### Differentiation

NOVA is not positioned as:

- a chatbot that ends with an answer;
- a project tool that requires structure before understanding intent;
- an autonomous agent that silently commits consequential actions;
- a dashboard that only reports status;
- a document generator disconnected from execution.

NOVA is a mission-first Enterprise Intelligence Platform in which conversation, structured execution, decisions, experts and deliverables share one traceable context.

## 2. Product Experience Principles

1. Start with the outcome.
2. Ask only what changes the path.
3. Show value before setup.
4. Keep one clear next action.
5. Reveal depth progressively.
6. Explain conclusions, uncertainty and consequences.
7. Let users correct NOVA at any time.
8. Preserve explicit human authority for critical decisions.
9. Make progress and blockers visible.
10. Use stable locations and consistent object names.
11. Make accessibility the default.
12. Turn work into usable, traceable deliverables.

## 3. Public Language Model

### Canonical public terms

| Internal term | Public term | Usage rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Program | Initiative or work | Use “initiative” only when a durable umbrella is necessary. |
| Mission / Mission Order | Work | Use “mission” only in brand/editorial contexts, not as required vocabulary. |
| PDS | Team | Never expose the acronym in V0 public views. |
| Gate | Review | Describe what is being reviewed. |
| Evidence | Sources and supporting information | “Evidence” may appear in legal, audit or expert detail. |
| Capability | What NOVA can do | Use capability only in admin/expert settings. |
| Baseline | Approved version | Use baseline only in expert technical view. |
| Artifact | File or deliverable | Match the actual object. |
| Escalation | Needs attention | Preserve “escalation” in expert operations views only. |
| Certification | Final review | Preserve certification where it has formal legal/governance meaning. |

### Voice

- Prefer verbs: Start, Review, Decide, Share, Export, Fix.
- Use sentences below 20 words where practical.
- State why information is needed.
- Avoid idioms, metaphors and unexplained acronyms.
- Do not anthropomorphize certainty: use “NOVA found” or “NOVA recommends,” not “I know.”
- Use calm, specific error language.
- Never use success color or celebratory motion for a high-risk approval.

## 4. Population and Persona Framework

V0 uses behavioral modes rather than demographic stereotypes. A person may move between modes by task.

### P-01 Guided Starter

- Includes: general public, novices, students, occasional VEEDDA beneficiaries.
- Goal: begin safely without learning the system.
- Needs: examples, clear questions, low choice count, reassurance, recovery.
- Default: Guided mode.

### P-02 Independent Contributor

- Includes: employees, students, project contributors, specialists.
- Goal: complete assigned or self-directed work efficiently.
- Needs: context, next actions, collaboration, deliverables.
- Default: Standard mode.

### P-03 Builder

- Includes: entrepreneurs, product creators, association leaders.
- Goal: turn ambiguity into a viable initiative.
- Needs: rapid framing, assumptions, milestones, practical outputs.
- Default: Standard mode with proactive clarification.

### P-04 Operational Lead

- Includes: managers, HR, finance, legal, CSE representatives, VEEDDA administrators.
- Goal: coordinate people, resolve exceptions and maintain control.
- Needs: ownership, risk, approvals, workload, auditability.
- Default: Standard mode with management views.

### P-05 Decision Authority

- Includes: executives, elected representatives, accountable public officials.
- Goal: make defensible decisions quickly.
- Needs: concise brief, options, consequences, dissent, sources, explicit approval.
- Default: Executive mode.

### P-06 Domain Expert

- Includes: IT, finance, legal, HR, researchers, engineers, expert advisors.
- Goal: inspect, challenge and contribute specialist judgment.
- Needs: traceability, exact assumptions, structured data, versioning.
- Default: Expert detail available.

### P-07 Platform Steward

- Includes: NOVA and VEEDDA administrators.
- Goal: govern access, policy, configuration and operational health.
- Needs: separate admin context, immutable audit, safe bulk actions.
- Default: Admin workspace; never mixed with personal work by accident.

### Accessibility overlays

Every persona can additionally require screen magnification, screen reader, keyboard-only input, voice input, reduced motion, increased contrast, simplified language, extended time or cognitive support. These are preferences and needs, not separate personas.

## 5. Jobs to Be Done

| ID | When… | I want to… | So I can… |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| JTBD-01 | I have an idea but no structure | describe the outcome in my words | see a credible path forward |
| JTBD-02 | Existing work is failing | show NOVA what exists and what is wrong | understand causes and recover control |
| JTBD-03 | I need reliable understanding | ask a research or analysis question | receive a traceable synthesis with limits |
| JTBD-04 | A consequential choice is approaching | compare options and consequences | make a defensible human decision |
| JTBD-05 | Work must move across people | assign, coordinate and follow actions | keep momentum without losing context |
| JTBD-06 | I need an executive-ready output | select audience and format | produce a professional deliverable |
| JTBD-07 | I am accountable for progress | see health, blockers and upcoming decisions | intervene only where needed |
| JTBD-08 | I disagree with NOVA | correct assumptions or request alternatives | remain in control of the outcome |
| JTBD-09 | I join ongoing work | understand purpose, state and my role | contribute without a long briefing |
| JTBD-10 | I need proof of how a result was reached | inspect sources, decisions and changes | verify or audit the outcome |

## 6. Mission Taxonomy

Publicly, the start screen uses outcome cards. Internally, each maps to one canonical mission type.

| Public choice | Canonical type | Typical outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create something | CREATE | New business, service, product, plan or initiative |
| Fix or recover work | RECOVER | Stabilized project, recovery plan, resumed execution |
| Understand a situation | ANALYZE | Research report, diagnosis, options, insight |
| Make a decision | DECIDE | Decision brief, recommendation, human decision record |
| Produce a deliverable | PRODUCE | Document, presentation, dashboard, export |
| Run and monitor work | EXECUTE | Coordinated plan, progress, exceptions, completion |
| Improve how we work | IMPROVE | Operating-model or process improvement |

Taxonomy SHALL not force selection before free-form input. NOVA may infer a type, explain it in plain language and allow correction.

## 7. Canonical Mission Object

Every item of work has one stable plain-language representation:

- Outcome: what success looks like.
- Context: why it matters and what already exists.
- People: owner, contributors, reviewers and decision authority.
- Plan: phases, actions and dependencies.
- Sources: files, links, data and prior work.
- Assumptions: what is believed but not established.
- Risks: what may prevent success.
- Decisions: choices requiring human judgment.
- Progress: completed, active, next and blocked.
- Deliverables: usable results and their lineage.

Internal governance identifiers may attach to this object but remain hidden outside Expert detail.

## 8. Outcome and Success Model

At setup, the user and NOVA agree on:

- intended outcome;
- audience or beneficiary;
- definition of done;
- constraints;
- deadline if meaningful;
- decision authority;
- acceptable autonomy level.

Success SHALL not be reduced to percent complete. NOVA reports:

- outcome confidence;
- completed results;
- unresolved assumptions;
- blocking risks;
- pending human decisions;
- deliverable readiness.

## 9. Scope Prioritization

### V0 required

- natural-language start;
- outcome clarification;
- mission canvas and plan preview;
- work home and work detail;
- progress and blocker monitoring;
- expert collaboration;
- decision package and explicit approval;
- deliverable creation, review and export;
- adaptive depth and Expert detail;
- accessibility and responsive behavior;
- source/assumption/decision traceability.

### Deferred beyond V0

- autonomous critical approval;
- visual workflow builder;
- marketplace;
- spatial, AR or VR interface;
- full cross-organization portfolio optimization;
- autonomous contractual or financial commitment;
- production administration implementation;
- high-fidelity brand and motion language.

## 10. Product Success Measures

Figma testing targets:

- 80% unassisted task-start success across representative cohorts;
- 90% correct identification of the current next action;
- 90% correct distinction between NOVA recommendation and human decision;
- 100% successful keyboard completion of prototype-supported flows;
- no critical WCAG 2.2 AA design violation;
- 80% comprehension of public labels without glossary help;
- 85% successful recovery from a seeded error;
- no accidental confirmation in critical-decision testing.
