# PROGRAM-018

# Master Research Program

## Document Status

DRAFT

## Purpose

This document defines the scientific research program governing all research laboratories operating under PROGRAM-018.

The Master Research Program SHALL coordinate, prioritize, challenge, consolidate and certify more than one hundred research axes across twelve research families.

No laboratory SHALL operate as an isolated research initiative. Every laboratory SHALL contribute to this common program and SHALL maintain explicit links between its research activities, findings, product implications and certification evidence.

---

# 1. Research Mission

The Master Research Program SHALL establish the scientific, behavioural, organizational, cognitive and technological foundations of the NOVA Mission Control Platform.

Its mission is to understand how humans think, work, decide, trust, collaborate, learn and receive complex information, then transform that knowledge into an Enterprise Intelligence Experience that is intuitive, accessible, calm, effective and capable of creating fundamentally new ways of working.

---

# 2. Research Operating Model

The Master Research Program SHALL:

- govern all PROGRAM-018 research laboratories;
- maintain a portfolio of more than one hundred research axes;
- assign each axis to one or more accountable laboratories;
- define research priorities and dependencies;
- require multidisciplinary review;
- distinguish demonstrated knowledge from unverified assumptions;
- translate findings into explicit product and experience implications;
- preserve high-potential ideas whose feasibility is not yet established;
- prevent duplication across laboratories;
- consolidate all findings into a common research evidence base.

Research families SHALL operate in parallel where possible and SHALL collaborate whenever an axis crosses disciplinary boundaries.

---

# 3. Research Families

## 3.1 Human Sciences

The Human Sciences research family SHALL include expertise from:

- cognitive psychology;
- neuroscience;
- ergonomics;
- occupational psychology;
- anthropology;
- sociology.

It SHALL investigate, at minimum:

- why some interfaces feel natural;
- why some interfaces create fatigue;
- why some experiences inspire trust;
- how cognition, perception, culture and social context shape interaction;
- how human limitations and capabilities SHALL influence NOVA.

## 3.2 Human Behaviour

The Human Behaviour research family SHALL observe how people actually work, rather than relying exclusively on declared preferences.

Representative populations SHALL include:

- executives;
- employees;
- students;
- retirees;
- entrepreneurs;
- software developers;
- human resources professionals;
- CSE representatives;
- private individuals.

Research SHALL examine real workflows, decisions, interruptions, workarounds, collaboration patterns, frustrations, expectations and environmental constraints.

## 3.3 Enterprise Research

The Enterprise Research family SHALL study the needs, structures, constraints and operating models of:

- small and medium-sized enterprises;
- mid-market companies;
- large enterprises;
- public administrations;
- associations;
- start-ups;
- hospitals;
- local and regional authorities.

Research SHALL identify both universal patterns and sector-specific requirements.

## 3.4 UX Benchmark

The UX Benchmark family SHALL study established platforms and products that have introduced new interaction models or working practices.

The benchmark SHALL include, but SHALL NOT be limited to:

- Microsoft;
- Apple;
- Google;
- Figma;
- Linear;
- Notion;
- Cursor;
- Arc Browser;
- Slack;
- Discord;
- Superhuman;
- Stripe;
- Vercel;
- GitHub.

The objective SHALL NOT be imitation. Research SHALL identify why specific innovations succeeded, which human needs they addressed, where their models fail and what NOVA can learn without reproducing existing products.

## 3.5 Cognitive Load Research

The Cognitive Load Research family SHALL measure the mental cost of every significant experience element, including:

- each click;
- each decision;
- each item of information;
- each color;
- each interruption.

Research SHALL establish measurable principles for minimizing unnecessary cognitive effort while preserving comprehension, control and informed decision making.

## 3.6 Trust Research

The Trust Research family SHALL investigate:

- why humans trust an artificial intelligence system;
- why trust is lost;
- how NOVA can explain its reasoning and decisions;
- how uncertainty SHALL be communicated;
- how users can verify, challenge and override NOVA;
- how confidence can be created without manipulation or false reassurance.

Trust SHALL be treated as a measurable and recoverable system property.

## 3.7 Future Interfaces

The Future Interfaces family SHALL investigate emerging and post-screen interaction models, including:

- voice;
- spatial computing;
- computer vision;
- augmented reality;
- virtual reality;
- eye tracking;
- gesture;
- multimodal interaction;
- autonomous and collaborative agents.

Research SHALL evaluate human value, accessibility, feasibility, safety and enterprise relevance rather than novelty alone.

## 3.8 Universal Accessibility

Universal Accessibility research SHALL extend beyond minimum WCAG conformance.

It SHALL investigate experiences adapted to:

- dyslexia;
- color vision deficiency;
- low vision and blindness;
- cognitive impairments;
- seniors;
- novices;
- expert users.

Accessibility SHALL be considered a foundational experience capability and not a corrective activity performed after design.

## 3.9 Emotional Design

The Emotional Design family SHALL define how NOVA can create:

- trust;
- calm;
- control;
- enjoyment;
- serenity.

NOVA SHALL be designed for emotional sustainability as well as operational efficiency.

Research SHALL prevent urgency, visual noise, uncertainty and artificial complexity from becoming default characteristics of enterprise work.

## 3.10 Knowledge Representation

The Knowledge Representation family SHALL investigate how NOVA can represent complex entities and relationships without overwhelming the user, including:

- a mission;
- a decision;
- a risk;
- an enterprise;
- a merger;
- a business plan.

Research SHALL explore progressive disclosure, multiple abstraction levels, narrative representations, visual models and role-adaptive views.

## 3.11 Deliverable Experience

The Deliverable Experience family SHALL determine how humans prefer to receive, understand, review and act upon results through formats including:

- PDF documents;
- PowerPoint presentations;
- dashboards;
- charts;
- video;
- executive summaries.

Research SHALL determine the appropriate format, depth, narrative and interaction model for each audience, context and decision.

## 3.12 Impossible Research

The Impossible Research family SHALL begin every investigation with the official question:

If no constraint existed, how would humans work?

Only after defining the unconstrained experience SHALL the research evaluate how it can become achievable.

This family SHALL deliberately challenge inherited software conventions, organizational habits, current technological limitations and assumptions presented as unavoidable facts.

# 4. Mandatory Research Result Classification

Every laboratory SHALL classify every material result into exactly one of the following categories.

## 4.1 Scientific Facts

Scientific Facts are findings supported by established evidence, reproducible research or sufficiently validated observation.

Each Scientific Fact SHALL include its evidence, source, scope, limitations and confidence level.

## 4.2 Hypotheses

Hypotheses are plausible propositions that remain to be verified.

Each Hypothesis SHALL include a validation method, expected evidence, falsification criteria and current confidence level.

## 4.3 Innovations

Innovations are original concepts, experience models or capabilities that do not yet exist in the required form.

Each Innovation SHALL include its intended human value, supporting research, risks, dependencies and proposed validation path.

## 4.4 Moonshots

Moonshots are ambitious ideas whose value may be transformational even when their feasibility, timing or implementation path remains uncertain.

Moonshots SHALL NOT be rejected solely because they cannot be implemented with current technology or operating constraints.

They SHALL be preserved, periodically reassessed and connected to scientific or technological developments that may enable them. A Moonshot identified today MAY become a foundational NOVA capability within five years.

---

# 5. Research Axis Governance

Every research axis SHALL define:

- a unique identifier;
- its research family;
- its accountable laboratory;
- its research question;
- its target populations and contexts;
- its methodology;
- its evidence requirements;
- ethical and accessibility considerations;
- dependencies on other axes;
- expected outputs;
- product and architecture implications;
- validation and certification criteria;
- its current status.

The Master Research Program SHALL maintain the canonical Research Axis Register and SHALL ensure that the portfolio contains more than one hundred active, planned or completed axes.

---

# 6. Laboratory Obligations

Every laboratory governed by this Master Research Program SHALL:

- operate from an approved laboratory charter;
- register all research axes before execution;
- document its methods and evidence;
- involve representative users where relevant;
- separate facts from hypotheses;
- identify innovations and Moonshots explicitly;
- disclose uncertainty, limitations and contradictory findings;
- produce actionable implications for product, UX, architecture and technology;
- participate in cross-laboratory synthesis;
- submit its results to independent review and certification.

---

# 7. Mandatory Outputs

The Master Research Program SHALL produce and maintain:

- Research Axis Register;
- Laboratory Portfolio;
- Scientific Facts Catalogue;
- Hypotheses Register;
- Innovation Portfolio;
- Moonshot Portfolio;
- Cross-Laboratory Dependency Map;
- Research Evidence Repository;
- Contradictions and Open Questions Register;
- Product Implications Report;
- Architecture Implications Report;
- Accessibility Implications Report;
- Research Synthesis Report;
- Research Certification Report.

---

# 8. Certification

No research family, laboratory or research axis SHALL be considered complete until:

- its evidence has been documented;
- its findings have been correctly classified;
- its limitations and contradictions have been disclosed;
- cross-laboratory dependencies have been reviewed;
- product implications have been assessed;
- independent review has been completed;
- formal certification has been issued.

No NOVA experience decision SHALL claim scientific support unless its supporting result is traceable to certified evidence governed by this Master Research Program.
