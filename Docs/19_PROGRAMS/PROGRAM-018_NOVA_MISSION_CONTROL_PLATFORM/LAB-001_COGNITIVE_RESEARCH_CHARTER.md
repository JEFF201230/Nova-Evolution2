# LAB-001

# Cognitive Research Laboratory Charter

## Laboratory Identifier

LAB-001

## Laboratory Name

Cognitive Research Laboratory

## Status

INITIATED

---

# Mission

The Cognitive Research Laboratory SHALL establish the scientific foundations governing every future NOVA interaction.

The laboratory SHALL study how humans naturally understand, discover, decide, learn, trust, collaborate and accomplish objectives.

No interface SHALL be designed before these cognitive foundations have been formally documented.

---

# Vision

NOVA SHALL adapt to human cognition.

Humans SHALL NOT adapt to NOVA.

---

# Primary Objective

Discover how to create the most intuitive Enterprise Intelligence Platform ever designed.

---

# Research Domains

The laboratory SHALL investigate, at minimum:

- Human cognition
- Attention
- Memory
- Decision making
- Mental workload
- Mental models
- Cognitive biases
- Human motivation
- Trust
- Curiosity
- Exploration
- Learning
- Error recovery
- Stress
- Collaboration
- Creativity
- Flow
- Problem solving
- Executive thinking
- Operational thinking
- Visual perception
- Information overload
- Progressive disclosure
- Human-AI collaboration

---

# Research Principle

Every recommendation SHALL be supported by:

- scientific literature;
- human-centered design principles;
- enterprise observations;
- measurable evidence;
- reproducible reasoning.

---

# Deliverables

The laboratory SHALL produce:

- Cognitive Principles Catalogue
- Mental Models Catalogue
- Decision Behaviour Catalogue
- Trust Model
- Cognitive Load Model
- Human Attention Model
- Information Consumption Model
- Human-AI Collaboration Model
- Executive Behaviour Model
- Operational Behaviour Model
- UX Design Implications Report
- Research Certification Report

---

# Success Criteria

The laboratory SHALL define the cognitive principles that every future NOVA interface SHALL follow.

No UI concept SHALL contradict the conclusions established by LAB-001.
