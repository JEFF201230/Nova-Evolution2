# PROGRAM-018

# Product Discovery Governance

## Status

DRAFT

## Purpose

This document defines the governance, expert organizations, user advisory panels, research laboratories, delivery pipeline and certification process governing PROGRAM-018.

No UX decision, interaction model, navigation model, design concept or Figma artefact SHALL be considered valid unless it has passed the governance defined in this document.

---

# Executive Steering Committee

Responsible for:

- strategic direction;
- product vision;
- executive arbitration;
- final approval.

---

# Product Strategy Board

Responsible for:

- product positioning;
- value proposition;
- market differentiation;
- product coherence.

---

# NOVA Design Lab

Responsible for:

- Product Discovery;
- UX innovation;
- interaction models;
- future concepts;
- experience architecture.

---

# Research Laboratories

The following laboratories SHALL operate in parallel:

- Cognitive Research Lab
- Behaviour Research Lab
- Enterprise Research Lab
- Cultural Research Lab
- Accessibility Research Lab
- Future Experience Lab
- AI Interaction Lab
- Visual Cognition Lab
- Information Architecture Lab
- Service Design Lab
- Impossible Lab

Each laboratory SHALL produce independent findings before synthesis.

---

# User Experience Advisory Board

The advisory board SHALL include representative users from:

- General Public
- Entrepreneurs
- Students
- Employees
- Managers
- Executive Leadership
- Human Resources
- Finance
- Legal
- IT
- Software Engineering
- UX Professionals
- Public Sector
- VEEDDA Beneficiaries
- VEEDDA Administrators
- CSE Representatives
- NOVA Platform Administrators

No major UX decision SHALL be approved without representative review.

---

# Independent Review Board

Responsible for challenging every assumption, identifying weaknesses, proposing alternatives and preventing design bias.

---

# Certification Office

Responsible for validating:

- research completeness;
- user relevance;
- architectural coherence;
- innovation;
- accessibility;
- feasibility;
- product differentiation.

---

# Mandatory Product Discovery Pipeline

Research

↓

Discovery

↓

Concept Definition

↓

Experience Architecture

↓

User Validation

↓

Independent Review

↓

Certification

↓

Figma V0

↓

Implementation

No phase SHALL be skipped.

---

# Exit Criteria

PROGRAM-018 Product Discovery SHALL be considered complete only after formal certification by the Certification Office.
