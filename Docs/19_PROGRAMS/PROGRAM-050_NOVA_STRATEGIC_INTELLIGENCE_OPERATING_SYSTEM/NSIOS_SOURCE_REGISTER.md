# NSIOS Source Register — État de l’art mondial

## 1. Purpose and research boundary

Ce registre consolide les méthodes publiquement observables utilisées par les cabinets de stratégie, cabinets d’audit, professionnels de l’investissement, standards internationaux et écoles de management. Il ne prétend pas reproduire leurs playbooks internes, formations propriétaires, bases payantes ou secrets professionnels.

La recherche a été arrêtée au **16 juillet 2026**. Une pratique n’est intégrée à NSIOS que si elle est publiquement documentée, cohérente avec plusieurs sources, ou clairement identifiée comme synthèse NOVA.

## 4. Due diligence taxonomy

| Diligence | Central question | Minimum dimensions | Typical output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial | Le marché et la position permettent-ils le plan ? | marché, clients, concurrence, pricing, GTM, forecast | CDD report / equity case |
| Market | Quelle demande accessible, à quel rythme et sous quelles contraintes ? | TAM/SAM/SOM, segments, drivers, regulation, scenarios | market fact base |
| Product | Le produit résout-il un besoin durable mieux que les alternatives ? | ICP/JTBD, adoption, retention, roadmap, moat | product diligence scorecard |
| Technology | La plateforme peut-elle soutenir la thèse ? | architecture, code, data, SDLC, debt, scale, team | TDD red flags and remediation cost |
| Cyber | Le risque résiduel est-il acceptable ? | governance, identify, protect, detect, respond, recover | risk register and target profile |
| Financial | Les résultats et cash sont-ils soutenables ? | QoE, revenue, margin, WC, debt-like, forecast | normalized earnings and bridge |
| Operational | L’organisation peut-elle capturer la valeur ? | process, capacity, cost, people, suppliers, execution | value creation plan |
| Vendor | Le vendeur peut-il présenter une base crédible et reliance-ready ? | fact book, normalization, risks, upside | VDD report and data room |
| Investment | Le rendement compense-t-il les risques au prix proposé ? | thesis, team, market, product, economics, valuation, exit | investment committee memo |

## 7. Source quality rules

| Tier | Source | Default confidence contribution |
|---|---|---|
| A | law, regulator, audited filing, official registry, signed contract, raw system record | high |
| B | official company documentation, investor material, standard body, peer-reviewed research | medium-high |
| C | reputable press, analyst methodology disclosed, expert interview with corroboration | medium |
| D | marketplace listing, comparison site, customer review, forum, anonymous testimony | low to medium |
| E | unsourced claim, generated summary, copied aggregator | inadmissible alone |

Tier is not truth. An official company source can be biased; a customer review can reveal a real failure. Confidence depends on provenance, independence, recency, directness, consistency and coverage.

## 8. Research limitations

- Public pages describe positioning better than internal execution detail.
- Proprietary frameworks cannot be reconstructed from marketing claims.
- School curricula change and public catalogs expose only part of teaching practice.
- Firm-authored success metrics may be selected and are not treated as independent evidence.
- Search visibility introduces geographic and language bias.
- Every future mission must refresh unstable facts rather than inherit this register as current market evidence.
