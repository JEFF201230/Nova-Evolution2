# NOVA V2 Investment Rationale

## Document Status

INVESTMENT CASE JUSTIFICATION.

## Purpose

This document explains why the revenue targets of USD 60M, USD 120M, and USD 150M are plausible or blocked, and which operating conditions would be required to achieve them.

## Why USD 60M

### Required Outcome

The base case requires roughly:

- 262 customers
- blended ARPA of USD 229k
- ARR of USD 60M
- services of USD 5.5M

### Why It Is Plausible

- NOVA Mission Ops solves a concrete enterprise problem: controlled, auditable AI-agent work.
- The market analysis points to strong enterprise demand for governed AI execution.
- The base case does not require market dominance; it requires a focused product in regulated and accountability-heavy segments.
- The average customer value remains within enterprise software norms when the product expands from team plans into enterprise and regulated contracts.
- NRR above 120% reduces the number of new logos needed.

### What Must Be True

- churn stays at or below 7%;
- upsell stays near 30% on retained accounts;
- sales cycles remain 3 to 9 months;
- direct services stay reusable;
- customer success keeps renewal quality high;
- product-market fit is real, not just demo enthusiasm.

### What Can Block It

- weak retention;
- high implementation friction;
- model cost overruns;
- inability to land enterprise security approvals;
- lack of clear ROI proof;
- Mission Ops UX too weak for repeated operational use.

## Why USD 120M

### Required Outcome

The ambitious case requires roughly:

- 360 customers
- blended ARPA of USD 333k
- ARR of USD 120M
- services of USD 8M

### Why It Is Plausible

- the product expands beyond Mission Ops into platform, governance, and enterprise cockpit use cases;
- higher-value regulated and enterprise contracts become common;
- on-prem/private cloud and security controls unlock larger deployments;
- developer and integration surfaces create stickiness;
- partner delivery increases reach.

### What Must Be True

- enterprise security and deployment are fully credible;
- the platform is broader than a single workflow tool;
- customer success and support are scaled;
- product adoption extends across multiple teams per customer;
- the model gateway and AI cost control are differentiated enough to win bigger contracts;
- the platform supports multiple product lines or packs.

### What Can Block It

- the company remains a single-surface product;
- marketplace and Studio are delayed too long;
- the organization cannot staff enterprise sales and customer success fast enough;
- the product is too dependent on services;
- the market sees NOVA as a niche tool rather than a platform.

## Why USD 150M

### Required Outcome

The exceptional case requires roughly:

- 400 customers
- blended ARPA of USD 375k
- ARR of USD 150M
- services of USD 10M

### Why It Is Plausible

- NOVA becomes a category leader in governed AI execution;
- partner and marketplace motions contribute meaningfully;
- multiple products or sector packs ship successfully;
- retention is extremely strong;
- expansion revenue is material;
- the platform is used across several business units per customer.

### What Must Be True

- NRR stays above 125% for several years;
- churn stays below 5% to 7%;
- the partner ecosystem scales without breaking quality;
- regulated enterprise and private deployment are a first-class commercial motion;
- the organization can support a much larger customer base without collapsing service quality.

### What Can Block It

- category ambiguity;
- too much dependence on manual delivery;
- weak partner controls;
- security or compliance failures;
- inability to scale model economics;
- no credible marketplace or sector pack strategy.

## Revenue Threshold Logic

| Threshold | Meaning | Main driver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USD 60M | Strong single-product enterprise execution business | Mission Ops product-market fit |
| USD 120M | Multi-product platform business | Enterprise expansion and platform breadth |
| USD 150M | Category leader with ecosystem leverage | Partner motion, marketplace, sector packs |

## Financial Interpretation

The three thresholds are not arbitrary:

- USD 60M is the point where the base product is commercially validated at scale.
- USD 120M is the point where the platform is clearly broader than mission execution alone.
- USD 150M is the point where NOVA behaves like a category platform with ecosystem leverage.

These outcomes are gated by the same levers:

- retention;
- expansion;
- services reuse;
- enterprise security;
- model economics;
- partner delivery;
- product breadth;
- sales capacity.

## Investment Recommendation

The right investment shape is staged:

1. Fund the Mission Ops MVP.
2. Prove paid pilots and enterprise renewals.
3. Fund the enterprise and cognitive platform only after retention and CAC are validated.
4. Fund marketplace and self-evolution only after platform economics and governance are proven.

This keeps capital allocation aligned with evidence instead of aspiration.
