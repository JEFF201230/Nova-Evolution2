# NOVA V2 Market Sizing

## Document Status

STRATEGIC MARKET MODEL.

## Purpose

This document sizes the market for NOVA as an enterprise governed AI execution platform. The goal is not precision theater. The goal is to produce a transparent sizing model that can be reproduced from the strategy pack.

## Market Definition

NOVA is positioned across overlapping categories:

- enterprise AI governance;
- workflow automation;
- agentic execution platforms;
- program and mission orchestration;
- enterprise observability and audit;
- AI-enabled productivity and decision support.

## TAM

### TAM Assumption

TAM = USD 180B

### TAM Formula

```
TAM = sum of adjacent spend categories relevant to governed AI execution
```

Directional category inputs:

- enterprise workflow automation
- AI governance and risk tooling
- AI software and orchestration platforms
- agentic execution tooling
- adjacent observability and integration spend

This model uses the midpoint of the directional range already stated in the strategy pack.

## SAM

### SAM Assumption

SAM = USD 18B

### SAM Formula

```
SAM = TAM x reachable_segment_share
reachable_segment_share = 10%
```

Why 10%:

- initial focus is regulated mid-market and enterprise teams;
- the first release is Mission Ops, not full general-purpose AI infrastructure;
- deployment and governance requirements limit immediate reach;
- the first wave should target buyers with the highest willingness to pay for accountability.

### SOM Formula

```
SOM = reachable customers x blended ARR per customer
```

## Target Markets

Priority target markets:

- professional services;
- software delivery organizations;
- regulated mid-market enterprises;
- public sector and associations;
- finance and insurance;
- legal operations;
- PMO and transformation offices;
- shared services functions.

## Segments

| Segment | Problem | Buyer value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executive operations | Need governed AI work with evidence | Accountability and speed |
| Program and PMO | Need portfolio and mission visibility | Less drift, better prioritization |
| Delivery teams | Need controlled AI assistance | Faster execution and fewer errors |
| Compliance / risk | Need audit and policy enforcement | Reduced shadow AI risk |
| Integrators / partners | Need extension contracts | Reusable commercial packaging |

## Positioning

Recommended positioning:

NOVA is the governed operating system for AI-assisted enterprise execution.

Positioning formula:

```
NOVA = AI execution + governance + evidence + cost control + enterprise integration
```

## Market Sizing Validation

The market sizing is internally consistent if:

- the product remains focused on governed execution rather than generic AI;
- the first release is Mission Ops, not a broad platform explosion;
- ARR per customer grows through mix shift and upsell;
- services remain a bridge, not the core business;
- partner and integration motions improve reach over time.
