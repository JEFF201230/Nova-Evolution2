# RED TEAM REVIEW CONTRACT

Status: `PROPOSED — MANDATORY FOR EVERY PHASE`

## Independence and scope

The Architecture Guardian reviews after implementation evidence and before certification. It does not repair its own findings or become a source of Business Evidence.

## Mandatory attacks

1. Locate any second owner, producer, repository, registry or current-record source.
2. Trace whether technical Mission/Runtime/Governance evidence enters Business Evidence.
3. Scan new product dependencies for CEREBRAU imports, calls, config or schemas.
4. Inspect persisted schemas for copied payloads or mutable owner status.
5. Attempt unavailable-authority/empty/default confusion.
6. Attempt Work-to-Evidence ownership transfer and contributor mutation through reads.
7. Attempt Intelligence creation/modification of Evidence, Action, Decision or Planning Priority.
8. Attempt to execute a Recommendation or expose a non-admitted proposal as Next Best Action.
9. Attempt Synthesis from Mission report, log, fixture or generated UI text.
10. Attempt Confidence from progress, readiness, pass counts, status or certification alone.
11. Compare protected Blueprint and PEOPLE/PLANNING/ACTIONS deltas/regressions.
12. Validate registry chain, contract/Blueprint resolution, certificate path and scope.

## Finding classes

- `BLOCKING_CRITICAL`: source/authority duplication, contamination, irreversible/protected drift.
- `BLOCKING_HIGH`: boundary or lifecycle violation capable of false business output.
- `REPAIRABLE`: local technical defect within the approved architecture.
- `NON_BLOCKING`: evidenced issue with no invariant impact and explicit disposition.
- `FALSE_POSITIVE`: disproven with cited evidence.

Any open blocking finding prevents certification. Repairable findings use the two-attempt budget and are re-reviewed.

## Required report

For each finding: ID, severity, `FACT`, `EVIDENCE`, exploit/counterexample, affected invariant, disposition, retest and residual limit. The report ends with `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `PASS_WITH_NON_BLOCKING_FINDINGS`; this is not a product certificate.
